⛏ Fool's Gold

A tiny game about Small Samples

You're an old prospector working 6 claims with a hunch and a pan. Each claim hides a true yield in gold-per-pan. Clear the profit line and it's worth staking; fall short and it's a bust.

You can't see what's down there — you can only pan to sample it. One pan is noisy; a few pans tighten your read. But pans are scarce: 15 pans across all 6 claims. Spend them where you're in doubt.

  • Call each claim RICH (stake it) or BUST (walk away).
  • Wager your confidence — right pays +100 × your bet, wrong costs the same.
  • Beware fool's gold: one flashy nugget in a small pan can scream "rich" over a worthless claim.

The whole lesson: small samples lie. A handful of pans — or a handful of data points — can look convincing and still be wrong, because a small sample swings wildly around the truth. The more you sample, the tighter your estimate gets. The skill is knowing when you've seen enough to trust a call, and saving your scarce pans for the claims that are genuinely in doubt — not the ones that were obvious all along.
